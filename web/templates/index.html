Turn handwritten ledgers into clean digital records

Photograph a sales book, stock sheet or receipt. We straighten and clean the image, read it with two OCR engines, and hand you an editable table to check before anything is saved.

PNG, JPEG, TIFF, BMP or WebP. Processing takes a few seconds.

How it works

  1. 01

    Capture

    A phone photo of a ledger page, stock sheet or receipt. No scanner needed.

  2. 02

    Clean

    OpenCV deskews, denoises, lifts faint ink with CLAHE and binarises the page.

  3. 03

    Read

    Tesseract maps the rows, EasyOCR reads the numbers, and fixed rules fuse the two.

  4. 04

    Review

    Correct any date, item, quantity, price or total, then save and export to CSV or JSON.
